The material present in this edition comes from four publications: _Sir Quixote of the Moors_, _Grey Weather_, _The Watcher by the Threshold_ (1902 and 1918 editions) and _The Moon Endureth_.

A few of the stories appear in more than one collection. In those cases, the later version was chosen, since the actual text is closer to versions later published in other sources.

The story “The Rime of True Thomas” appeared in _Grey Weather_ as “The Moor-Song.” The later, renamed version was chosen for this edition.

A story present in the _The Watcher by the Threshold_ (1918), “Divus Johnson,” was not included in this edition because it was later incorporated in another of Buchan’s works, _The Runagates Club_.

In “Sir Quixote of the Moors” there was an extra, unauthorized paragraph at the end of the text. It was removed in this edition.

“Streams of Water in the South” was published both in _Grey Weather_ and _The Moon Endureth_. The earlier version in _Grey Weather_ has two paragraphs not present in the later _The Moon Endureth_ version; these are not present here. In the same story, some quoted passages were missing quotes. Later editions of this story show the proper quotation marks. Appropriate quotation marks were added to this edition.
