The material present in this edition comes from four publications: _Sir Quixote of the Moors_, _Grey Weather_, _The Watcher by the Threshold_ and _The Moon Endureth_.

The following stories come from _Grey Weather_:

- “Prester John”
- “Politics and the May-Fly”
- “A Reputation”
- “A Journey of Little Profit”
- “At the Rising of the Waters”
- “The Earlier Affection”
- “The Black Fishers”
- “Summer Weather”
- “The Oasis in the Snow”
- “The Herd of Standlan”
- “Comedy in the Full Moon”

The following stories come from the 1918 edition of _The Watcher by the Threshold_:

- “No-Man’s Land”
- “The Far Islands”
- “The Watcher by the Threshold”
- “The Outgoing of the Tide”
- “Basilissa”
- “The King of Ypres”

“Fountainblue” comes from the 1902 ediion of _The Watcher by the Threshold_. Another story present in the collection, “Divus Johnson,” was not included in this edition because it was later incorporated in another of Buchan’s works, _The Runagates Club_.

The following stories come from _The Moon Endureth_:

- “The Company of the Marjolaine”
- “A Lucid Interval”
- “The Lemnian”
- “Space”
- “Streams of Water in the South”
- “The Grove of Ashtaroth”
- “The Riding of Ninemileburn”
- “The Kings of Orion”
- “The Green Glen”
- “The Rime of True Thomas” (Published in _Grey Weather_ as “The Moor-Song”)

In “Sir Quixote of the Moors” there was an extra, unauthorized paragraph at the end of the text. It was removed in this edition.

In “Streams of Water in the South” some quotes passages were missing quotes. Later editions of this story show the proper quotation marks. Appropriate quotation marks were added to this edition.
